Language setting, a new calendar, and a News tab in notifications!

• Choose Korean or English in Settings > Language. If your device is in English, the app starts in English
• The Calendar tab now shows just the month. Tap a date and the calendar folds to that week with the day's routines and to-dos below; swipe sideways to move between weeks
• Notifications are split into [My alerts | News]. Feature and update news lives in the News tab, and alerts can be swiped away
• Getting started now points at the actual buttons on screen, step by step. The first mission is "complete one routine today"
• Tap + next to a category and an input opens right there — just type to add a to-do
• Routine Runner keeps speeding up after 40 seconds. Go for a new best
• If your email is already registered with a different sign-in method, we let you know
• Fixed sideways swipes sometimes getting stuck on the House tab, and the keyboard covering the nickname field on first launch (Android)
