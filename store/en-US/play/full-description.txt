Keep your routines and your room grows!

Rougether is a routine tracker for habits that fizzle out when you go it alone — you and your friends live in one house and keep your routines together.

■ Today at a glance
• Check off routines and to-dos and build your streak
• Add routines and to-dos right from your room or the calendar. Pick a category later if you like
• Start small with a routine that matches your interests
• Set weekday repeats and reminder times however you like
• Import events from your device calendar as to-dos. We only read your calendar, never write to it

■ A room that grows as you keep going
• Complete routines to earn coins and collect furniture from the gacha
• Decorate your room freely with what you collect. Your room is your record
• Choose a character to live in your room with you

■ Mini-games in your room
• Play obstacle jump, stair climb, and number merge
• Chase the all-member leaderboard with your best score in each game

■ Better together
• Build a house with friends, visit each other's rooms, and send cheers
• Complete shared missions to level up your house
• Look back on your week with the weekly review: completion rate, by day and by routine
• A room left empty for too long gets cobwebs. Clear them for coins
• Leave a note in each other's guestbooks

■ Never forget
• Get reminders at your routine and to-do times
• Turn each notification type on or off in Settings
